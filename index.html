Teaching machines to design biomaterials

AIMS, at the School of Physics, Suranaree University of Technology, combines artificial intelligence with first-principles modeling to discover, understand and predict new materials.

Where physics meets data

We are a research group in computational condensed matter physics. We run DFT simulations on supercomputers, train deep learning models on the results, and check both against real spectroscopy and experiments.

Research directions

Five themes, one method: physics-based models for understanding, machine learning for speed.

01

ML screening of dopants

Predicting how cationic dopants change the elastic and structural behavior of hydroxyapatite, using large databases and cross-checking against DFT elastic tensors.

HydroxyapatiteBiomaterialsDFT
02

High-entropy perovskites

Electronic, optical and polarization properties of (Ba,Sr,Ca,Bi,Na)TiO3 with VASP, studied under strain for piezo-photocatalysis.

VASPSQSPhotocatalysis
03

Spectroscopy meets AI

Core-level binding energy shifts and X-ray absorption spectra as fingerprints of local structure, inverted by neural networks with calibrated uncertainty.

XPSXASFEFFPyTorch
04

Energy market forecasting

Walk-forward benchmarks of tree-based and deep models for day-ahead and imbalance prices on the Japan Electric Power Exchange.

LightGBMLSTMJEPX
05

Medical image deep learning

Classification systems for diabetic retinopathy from fundus images, turning clinical data into screening support.

CNNFundus imagingHealthcare
